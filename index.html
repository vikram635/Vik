FAQ

Common questions

How do I place an order?

Fill the order form on this page with your name, WhatsApp number, project type and details. You will get a tracking link to follow your order.

How do I pay?

Open your tracking link and pay by UPI using the button, the UPI ID or the QR code. After paying, enter the transaction ID in the box so we can verify it.

Can I ask for changes after delivery?

Yes. Open your tracking link and use the Need changes? box to tell us what you want changed.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. If you have a coupon code, enter it in the order form. After your delivery you can also get a referral code to share with friends.

How do I get my finished edit?

When it is ready, your tracking page shows a download button.
